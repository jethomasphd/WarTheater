# Paper 2 — v2: Killed Fast, Left Fragile, Counted Short

**A three-finding, student-authored edition of Paper 2, built around the findings
its first author chose to develop.**

This is a focused re-narration of [Paper 2 — *The Direct Toll Is a
Floor*](../README.md). The parent paper reports eight findings across the full
behavioral-statistics toolkit. After reading the parent, the first author (a West
African public-health nurse) identified three that "feel like one argument," and
this edition is built around exactly those three:

> **The war killed fast and early; the health system gradually lost its ability
> to absorb further shocks; and the deaths we can count may only represent part
> of the real human toll.**

| Movement | Parent finding | Claim | Tool |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finding 1 | Mortality was **front-loaded** — 79.6% of deaths in the first 23.5% of days | means comparison |
| 2 | Finding 5 | The system's **resilience eroded** — the marginal cost of a strike rose ~6× as facility damage accumulated | moderation |
| 3 | Finding 6 | The documented toll is a **floor** — ~15,800–18,200 total in Iran at the 4:1 literature ratio | scenario projection |

**Nothing is re-estimated.** This edition reads the *same* frozen v1.2 dataset
the parent uses and runs the parent's own computations, so every statistic here
matches the parent exactly. What is new is the framing — three findings assembled
into one causal-sounding story about how a health system fails under sustained
air war, read from the clinical side.

## Reproduce everything (~30 seconds)

```bash
cd ResearchData/Paper2/v2
python3 -m pip install -r requirements.txt   # pandas, numpy, scipy, statsmodels, matplotlib
bash run_all.sh                              # panel + register + focused tables + 3 figures
```

The analysis is pinned to the frozen `../../releases/v1.2/iranwar_event_dataset.csv`
(the same release the parent paper uses), so it reproduces identically regardless
of later dataset releases. All computation is seeded (`SEED=42`). The final step,
`src/04_synthesis.py`, re-derives every headline number and **asserts** it against
its manuscript value — upstream drift fails loudly.

## Headline findings (all regenerated by `run_all.sh`)

| # | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The war **killed fast and early** | 79.6% of documented deaths in the 23.5% of days that were Major Combat; phase η² = 0.76; combat-vs-ceasefire Hedges *g* = 3.4; per-strike lethality collapsed 15.4 → 0.7 (`t1_*`, fig1) |
| 5 | The system **lost its ability to absorb shocks** | strike × facility-damage interaction *p* = .006; simple slopes 0.18 → 0.63 → 1.07 (~6× steepening); tracks the damage curve, not a generic late-war shift (phase-interaction *p* = .89) (`t5_*`, fig2) |
| 6 | The counted dead are only a **floor** | Iran 3,166–3,636 documented → 15,830–18,180 total at 4:1; Lebanon 2,993–4,308 → 14,965–21,540; Bonji intake alone cut water to ~10,000 people (`t6_*`, fig3) |

## Relationship to the parent paper

This edition is a **subset re-narration**, not a competing analysis. It is the
right entry point for a reader who wants the health-system collapse story in three
moves; the parent paper is the right entry point for the full evidentiary record
(the civilian-facing targeting result, the mediation, the source-divergence and
confidence-weighting robustness, and the two lethality findings in full). Cite the
parent for the complete study; cite this edition for the three-finding argument
and its first-author framing.
