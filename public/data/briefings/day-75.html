Beijing Becomes the Iran Summit; Hormuz Geometry Congeals

Trump separately announced on May 11 that he will discuss U.S. arms sales to Taiwan with Xi, a departure from the longstanding Six Assurances posture; his May 12 framing — “He [Xi] will bring up Taiwan, I think more than I will” — pre-positions the dual-leverage geometry.

The G2 framing Al Jazeera and CNBC adopted for the summit is the most charitable reading; the more cynical read is that this is a managed-conflict summit dressed as a peace summit.

The Hormuz geometry has now congealed into a stable dysfunction.
