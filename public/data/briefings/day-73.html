Gulf Triple-Strike: IRGC Operationalizes the Sub-Strike Threshold

Three Gulf states were hit by drones in a single news cycle for the first time since April 5, and the pattern is precisely calibrated. Kuwait got an unattributed dawn incursion that air defenses intercepted — the first KW airspace breach since the April 8 ceasefire, and the second this year (April 25 precedent: two drones from Iraq hit northern KW border posts). UAE got two attack drones launched from Iranian territory, which UAE MoD shot down and, crucially, directly attributed to Tehran — the first such direct attribution of the war (the May 4 and May 8 UAE salvos were attributed to “Iran or Iran-aligned”; this one is named). Qatar got a drone strike on a bulk carrier 23 nm NE of Doha inside Qatari territorial waters — the most significant Gulf maritime attack inside Qatari waters of the entire war — with no casualties, no environmental impact, and a vessel that reached port under its own power. The targeting envelope tells a story: enough kinetic event to communicate, not enough to demand U.S. retaliation as a NATO-Article-5 analog.
