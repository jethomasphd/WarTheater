Trump-Xi Day 1: A Joint Hormuz Position Emerges

Layered on top of these substantive outcomes were the optics that diplomatic protocol partisans had been watching for: Xi descended the Great Hall stairs personally to shake hands with Trump, then individually greeted each member of a seventeen-person U.S. business delegation. Xi opened the substantive session with a Thucydides Trap reference, asking whether the United States and China could escape the historical pattern in which a rising power and ruling power produce war. The session framework was Beijing’s official English formulation: “a constructive China-U.S. relationship of strategic stability” — Xi declared that Beijing would treat that phrase as guidance “for the next three years and beyond.”

The Iran sub-track is now structurally Chinese. Taiwan was the other half of Xi’s Day 1 substance, and the contrast with the cooperative Hormuz language was unmistakable.
